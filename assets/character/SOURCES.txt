The actual free archive contains Superhero Male/Female, NOT the teen models
shown in the pack overview. The teen bodies are in the paid Source edition.
Skog uses the male head, eyes and eyebrows with a shortened lower face,
rounder proportions and enlarged cranium. Hair, hat, body, coat, backpack,
hands, boots and lantern remain Skog's original geometry. This is an adapted
hybrid character, not the paid Quaternius teen model.

source-base.gltf and Superhero_Male_FullBody.bin retain the original geometry,
rig and material metadata used by the extractor. The unused full-body/hair
textures are not included; this source is not a self-contained textured model.
T_Eye_Brown.png is the original eye texture, embedded unchanged at runtime.
Skin uses Skog's material and subtle vertex colour; no body texture is shipped.

UAL1_Standard.glb.gz is the unmodified source GLB compressed with gzip.
Selected clips: Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop. The build script samples
world-space limb directions and foot trajectories from the original rig.
Skog retargets them to its smaller child, blends locomotion, constrains the
lantern arm and applies terrain foot placement. Movement remains driven by
player.walk (3.5 radians/metre), shared with the recorded footsteps.

REBUILD (Bun, no download required):
  bun scripts/build_character_assets.js

quaternius-character.js is the generated runtime data. Original source files
are only for editing/rebuilding and are never loaded by the game.
